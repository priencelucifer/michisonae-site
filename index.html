MichiSonae

Quiet road and vehicle warnings while you drive.

An Android app that spots potholes with the phone's motion sensors, warns you by voice about hazards other drivers found, and, with an optional read-only OBD-II adapter, keeps an eye on your car's battery, fault codes and fuel. No account, and no route history.
